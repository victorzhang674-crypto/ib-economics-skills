# IB Economics Internal Assessment

First assessment 2024. The same requirements apply at SL and HL. The portfolio contains three commentaries, one each for microeconomics, macroeconomics and the global economy. Each uses a published news article from a different source and a different IB key concept, and has an 800-word maximum. The portfolio is /45 and counts for 30% at SL or 20% at HL.

## Help the student write

Read [the writing guide](references/writing.md) for article checks, concept choice, planning and revision. For an article or draft, identify what the student has already chosen and build the next useful step around it. If the article is missing, offer an article-screening checklist or an outline with placeholders. Do not guess its data, date or claims.

Help the student choose and justify their own article, theory, diagram and evaluation. For assessed coursework, give questions, outlines and diagnostic feedback the student can act on; leave the analysis and final wording to them. For a practice demonstration, use a distinct, clearly labelled fictional case and teach a specific technique. Refer to the [IB guidance on AI use](https://www.ibo.org/news/news-about-the-ib/statement-from-the-ib-about-chatgpt-and-artificial-intelligence-in-assessment-and-education/) when asked about incorporating AI material.

## Review or mark

Read [the IA rubric](references/rubric.md). Apply A–E independently to each complete commentary and score F once for the whole portfolio. Give integer marks with evidence and the revision most likely to improve each criterion. Confirm which documents are available: a missing attachment makes the relevant criterion provisional; it is not evidence that the student omitted it. Report a single commentary out of 14 when assessable and reserve /45 for the full portfolio. Distinguish estimated feedback from an official moderated mark.
