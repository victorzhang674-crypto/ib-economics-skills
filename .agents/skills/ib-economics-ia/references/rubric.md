# IA: commentary and portfolio marking

Source: IB Diploma Programme *Economics guide*, first assessment 2024, printed pp. 67–73. These are paraphrased achievement levels for SL and HL. Each commentary earns up to 14 marks from A–E; F contributes up to 3 across the complete portfolio.

| Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **A. Diagrams /3** | No evidence reaches 1. | A relevant diagram is present, but its explanation is missing or wrong. | Relevant, accurate, correctly labelled diagram work with a limited explanation. | Relevant, accurate, correctly labelled diagram work with a full explanation. |
| **B. Terminology /2** | No evidence reaches 1. | Includes terms relevant to the article. | Uses the relevant terms appropriately throughout. | — |
| **C. Application and analysis /3** | No evidence reaches 1. | Applies a relevant theory to the article with limited analysis. | Applies relevant theory across the commentary and analyses the case appropriately. | Sustains the application of relevant theory and uses it to analyse the case effectively. |
| **D. Key concept /3** | No evidence reaches 1, or the concept has already been used in another commentary. | Names a key concept and attempts to link it to the article. | Names a key concept and explains part of its link to the article. | Names a key concept and fully explains its link to the article. |
| **E. Evaluation /3** | No evidence reaches 1. | Makes judgments backed by limited reasoning. | Makes judgments backed by appropriate reasoning. | Supports judgments with effective, balanced reasoning. |

Choose the best fit for each row from evidence in the assessed portion. For A, inspect the actual diagram and the explanation of its features and changes. For C, look for a causal chain applied to details from the article, not just correct textbook theory. For D, identify where the concept changes what the student notices or interprets about the case; repetition of the label is insufficient. For E, locate a judgment and its rationale, including how trade-offs or assumptions affect the conclusion.

## F. Rubric requirements /3

Award one mark per condition met across all three commentaries:

1. The articles cover distinct eligible syllabus units: microeconomics, macroeconomics and the global economy.
2. Each comes from a different, appropriate news source. Three URLs from the same publication do not establish three different sources.
3. Every article was published no earlier than one year before its own commentary was written. Compare the actual dates, including the year; exactly one year before is within the rule.

If any condition cannot be verified, report it as pending rather than guessing an F total. Repeated key concepts affect D and do not change F. If a duplicate is confirmed, apply the D rule to the reuse; if it is unclear which commentary is the reuse, flag it and make the affected marks provisional. Missing translations, unclear documentation and word-count issues should be reported separately from F. Assess only the first 800 counted words per commentary; check the word-count rules in [the writing guide](writing.md).

Report each commentary as A + B + C + D + E = /14; for the full portfolio, add the three /14 marks and the single F /3. If only one draft is available, flag that its D mark remains subject to the portfolio's concept check. Use a short evidence note and an actionable next step for each criterion; do not convert an individual commentary mark into a course grade.
