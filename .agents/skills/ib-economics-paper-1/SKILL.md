# IB Economics Paper 1

First assessment 2024. SL and HL choose one question from three and answer both parts: (a) /10 and (b) /15. Time: 1 hour 15 minutes, plus five minutes of reading time. No calculator. Weight: 30% SL, 20% HL. All four syllabus units are eligible; HL can include extension material. Confirm level when it affects topic coverage or weighting.

## Mark a response

Read [the Paper 1 markbands](references/marking.md). Use the exact question, the student's answer and any diagrams, and the matching exam markscheme if available. Score the parts separately by best fit using their respective bands and question-specific guidance. Give a band and integer mark for each assessable part, cite concrete evidence, explain what keeps it from the next band, and name the most useful revision. Label guide-only scores as estimates. If the question or a referenced diagram is missing, give the feedback the evidence supports and flag what remains provisional. Report /25 for a full answer, or the part's own maximum for a partial review.

## Plan or write a practice answer

- **(a) Explain:** Parse every demand and scope limit. Choose the relevant model and explain each causal step from the initial change to the requested result. Use terms in context. Where appropriate, draw the diagram with accurate axes, curves and equilibrium labels, then explain the changes it shows. Check that each cause, effect, or comparison requested is covered. This part does not have an evaluation requirement.
- **(b) Evaluate or discuss:** Plan a direct answer, the theory supporting it, and a specific real-world example that helps test the claim. Connect the example to a model and a trade-off: for instance, explain why elasticity, time lags, or stakeholder exposure changes the outcome in this case. Weigh competing effects and reach a judgment responsive to the command term. A named country or policy alone does not develop the example. Verify case details when needed and distinguish a hypothetical illustration from a real-world example.
- For a timed plan, a starting split is 30 minutes for (a), 40 for (b), and 5 to check. For a practice question or model answer, label original material as practice and make the marking basis clear.
